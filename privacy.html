Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short

Glimmer does not collect any data about you. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What Glimmer stores, and where

To work, Glimmer keeps a few things only on your device: your settings (such as your screen-free time and wake-up time), your evenings (the rituals you chose, how tense you felt, when you put the phone away, short looks at the phone during the night, and your morning answer), and Glimmer's progress (points, moons, moon dust, colours and places).

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. If your iPhone makes backups to iCloud or a computer, the data is part of that backup, handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Sensors

At night Glimmer uses the motion sensor to notice when the phone is turned face down or picked up, and the proximity sensor to switch the screen off while it lies face down. These readings are used in the moment and are not stored, except for the time of a short look, which stays on your phone.

Notifications

Evening reminders are local notifications, scheduled by your phone itself. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in Glimmer's settings or in the iOS Settings app.

Widget

The Glimmer widget shows a picture of Glimmer and a short line of text. The app saves these in a shared folder on your phone so the widget can read them. They never leave the device.

Purchases

Glimmer Pro is sold as a subscription through Apple's App Store. Payment, billing and your Apple account are handled entirely by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Glimmer only asks Apple, on your phone, whether a subscription is active. We do not receive your name, payment details or purchase history.

Sharing

When you tap a share button (for example your week or your evening type), Glimmer creates the picture on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Glimmer under Settings → Delete all data, or simply delete the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing left anywhere else.

Children

Glimmer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Glimmer will never start collecting data without asking you first.
