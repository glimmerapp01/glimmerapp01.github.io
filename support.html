How does the night work?

In the evening, tap to put your phone away and turn it face down. Glimmer falls asleep and the screen switches off. If you pick the phone up during the night, Glimmer notices a short look. That is never a penalty. In the morning you tell Glimmer how the evening went.

Should I keep Auto-Lock on?

Yes. iOS doesn't let apps lock the phone, so keep Auto-Lock on (Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock). Glimmer still knows you were away and counts your night.

I don't get the evening reminder

Check that reminders are on in Glimmer's settings and that notifications for Glimmer are allowed in the iOS Settings app. A Focus mode can also hold notifications back.

I forgot the morning check-in

No problem. Glimmer asks about a forgotten evening the next time you open it, for up to a week.

How do I restore Glimmer Pro?

Open Glimmer, go to the Pro page and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Glimmer. You can also tap Manage subscription in Glimmer's settings. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple account.

Will I lose Glimmer's progress?

Your progress lives only on your phone. It is kept in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), so a restored backup brings Glimmer back. Deleting the app deletes the progress.
